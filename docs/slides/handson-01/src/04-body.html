Mass, drag, rolling resistance

Point at: the two Constant blocks on the left. Rolling resistance takes its coefficient (0.012) and the road inclination (0 — flat) as inputs, not parameters. Unconnected, the model is incomplete.

Say: drag and rolling push against the ground, not against the car — their supports go to ground.

The one parameter that bites

parameter m::Dyad.Mass = 1400
parameter CdA::Area = 0.63
parameter rho::Density = 1.2
parameter v_nominal::Velocity = 30
…
drag = QuadraticSpeedDependentForce(ForceDirection = false,
    v_nominal = v_nominal, f_nominal = -0.5 * rho * CdA * v_nominal ^ 2)
rolling = RollingResistance(fWeight = m * g)
  • Each value is a parameter of Body; the parts use the names.
  • f_nominal is ½ρCdA·v² at the nominal speed.
  • Negative makes the source a load rather than a push.
  • ForceDirection = false makes it oppose motion both ways.

They get wrong: a positive f_nominal. The car then accelerates forever — drag pushes it. If a student's car in step 05 goes to absurd speeds, this sign is the first thing to check.

Ask the room: why parameters instead of 340.2 and 13729? (The next reader sees ρ, CdA and m, not magic numbers — and m feeds the mass and the rolling resistance, so changing the car's weight changes both or neither.)
