Ask the room first: before showing the plot, how much slower will the car be? Most guess a lot.

Say: seen through the rolling radius, 1 kg·m² behaves like J / r² = 1 / 0.31² ≈ 10 kg of extra mass on a 1400 kg car. Inertia slows the launch, not the terminal speed — at constant speed nothing accelerates, so inertia does nothing.
