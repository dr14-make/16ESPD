# How you pass

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8">
<div>

### 1 · Take part in class

<div class="text-sm">

Attendance is not compulsory. But **active attendance and participation** — questions, answers, work in the practicals — earn **points that count toward the exam**.

</div>

### 2 · Practice — build a vehicle simulation

<div class="text-sm">

- Everyone works **on their own**.
- Your work lives in your own **GitHub** repository.
- Each task is delivered as a **pull request**.

</div>

</div>
<div>

### 3 · Final exam

<div class="text-sm">

- Multiple-choice test, about **30 questions**.
- An oral exam if you are not satisfied with the result.

> **No presentation** There is no student presentation in this course. Your participation, your practical work and the test are the assessment.

</div>

</div>
</div>
